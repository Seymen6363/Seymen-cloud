# Seedance 2.0 – Shot-by-Shot-Prompts für den Moè Shop

Vier Videos, passend zu den Video-Feldern im Theme. Jeder Block unter „PROMPT“ wird **komplett** in Seedance 2.0
eingefügt. Lade immer dein **echtes Produktfoto als Referenzbild** hoch, damit Form, Farbe und Etikett stimmen.

Allgemeine Einstellungen: 1080p, ohne Ton, 24 fps, Kamera-Bewegung „smooth“.
Export für Shopify: MP4 (H.264), unter 20 MB.

Ruhige Marke = wenige, saubere Effekte. Die Videos laufen als Hintergrund in Schleife, deshalb kein Text,
keine harten Schnitte, kein Flackern. Der letzte Frame muss bei den Loops (1 und 4) dem ersten entsprechen.

---

## 1 · Hero-Loop – `hero-loop.mp4`
Theme-Feld: **Moè Hero → Hintergrund-Video** · Format 16:9 · Dauer 10 s · nahtloser Loop

### PROMPT
```
Seamless 10-second looping product film, 16:9, no text, no people. Handmade grey-green clay soap bar (exactly as in the reference image) resting on a dark wet river stone, sprigs of eucalyptus and fresh mint around it, soft morning daylight from the right, warm cream and sage-green grade, shallow depth of field, lots of calm negative space on the left third of the frame.

SHOT 1 (00:00-00:04) — Slow Push-In
• EFFECT: Slow dolly-in + shallow depth of field
• Camera starts wide at soap height and glides toward the bar at a constant, very slow speed (frame scale grows by roughly 8% over the shot)
• Small water droplets slide down the side of the soap in real time
• Eucalyptus leaves in the foreground sway gently in a light breeze, softly out of focus
• Continuous shot, no cut

SHOT 2 (00:04-00:07) — Light Sweep
• EFFECT: Moving light rays + slow-motion water droplet (approximately 25% speed)
• A soft beam of sunlight drifts slowly across the soap surface from right to left, revealing the fine mineral texture
• One single water drop falls from a mint leaf onto the stone beside the soap in slow motion, creating a tiny clean splash
• This is the SIGNATURE VISUAL EFFECT — the slow-motion drop under the moving light
• Camera keeps the same slow forward drift, no new movement

SHOT 3 (00:07-00:10) — Return to Loop Point
• EFFECT: Slow dolly-out (reverse of shot 1) + gentle focus breathing
• Camera eases back out to the exact opening framing, decelerating smoothly
• Light returns to its starting position, foreground leaves settle
• The final frame matches the first frame so the clip loops invisibly
```

### MASTER EFFECTS INVENTORY
1. Slow dolly-in / dolly-out — used 2x (shots 1, 3) — calm breathing movement that makes the loop seamless
2. Shallow depth of field / focus breathing — used 2x (shots 1, 3) — keeps the soap as the only sharp subject
3. Moving light rays — used 1x (shot 2) — reveals texture, adds premium feel
4. Slow-motion (~25 %) water drop — used 1x (shot 2) — signature moment
5. Ambient foliage motion — used 3x (all shots) — keeps the frame alive without distracting from the headline

### EFFECTS DENSITY MAP
```
00:00-00:04 = LOW DENSITY (dolly-in, shallow DOF — 2 effects in 4s)
00:04-00:07 = MEDIUM DENSITY (light sweep, slow-motion drop, drift — 3 effects in 3s)
00:07-00:10 = LOW DENSITY (dolly-out, focus breathing — 2 effects in 3s)
```

### ENERGY ARC
Act 1: Calm entry — the viewer is drawn slowly toward the product.
Act 2: A single quiet highlight (light sweep + falling drop) gives the eye something to catch on.
Act 3: Everything settles back to the start, so the loop never shows a seam behind the headline text.

## 2 · Tonerde-Seife im Schaum – `tonerde-schaum.mp4`
Theme-Feld: **Video + Text (Tonerde) → Video** · Format 4:5 · Dauer 8 s

### PROMPT
```
8-second vertical 4:5 skincare product video, no text, no faces. Close-up of two hands with natural skin and short clean nails holding the grey-green clay soap bar from the reference image above a matte cream ceramic basin. Warm bathroom light, linen towel and a small green plant softly blurred in the background, warm cream and sage grade, editorial skincare look.

SHOT 1 (00:00-00:02) — Water On
• EFFECT: Slow-motion (approximately 40% speed) + rack focus
• A clear stream of water runs over the soap held in both hands
• Focus pulls from the water stream in the foreground onto the soap surface
• Static camera, slightly above the hands

SHOT 2 (00:02-00:05) — Lather Build
• EFFECT: Slow push-in + real-time texture
• Hands rotate the bar slowly; a dense, creamy, slightly grey-tinted lather forms between the fingers
• Camera pushes in gently toward the foam (scale increases about 10%)
• Small bubbles catch the warm light and sparkle

SHOT 3 (00:05-00:07) — Foam Macro
• EFFECT: Extreme macro + slow-motion (approximately 25% speed)
• Very close view of the foam: fine bubbles slowly expanding and popping, mineral clay particles visible in the lather
• This is the SIGNATURE VISUAL EFFECT — the macro texture of the clay foam
• Transition: soft defocus blur leads into the final shot

SHOT 4 (00:07-00:08) — Rest
• EFFECT: Focus return + static hold
• The soap bar lies on a wooden soap dish next to the basin with a few drops of water, sharp and calm
• Static final frame, no movement
```

### MASTER EFFECTS INVENTORY
1. Slow-motion — used 2x (shots 1, 3) — makes water and foam look rich and tactile
2. Rack focus / focus return / defocus transition — used 3x (shots 1, 3, 4) — guides the eye and connects the shots softly
3. Slow push-in — used 1x (shot 2) — builds closeness to the product
4. Extreme macro — used 1x (shot 3) — signature texture moment
5. Static hold — used 1x (shot 4) — clean product end frame

### EFFECTS DENSITY MAP
```
00:00-00:02 = MEDIUM DENSITY (slow-motion, rack focus — 2 effects in 2s)
00:02-00:05 = LOW DENSITY (push-in — 1 effect in 3s)
00:05-00:07 = MEDIUM DENSITY (macro, slow-motion, defocus transition — 3 effects in 2s)
00:07-00:08 = LOW DENSITY (static hold — 1 effect in 1s)
```

### ENERGY ARC
Act 1: Water introduces freshness and motion.
Act 2: The lather builds up to the macro foam, the most sensory moment.
Act 3: A calm product shot leaves the viewer with the soap itself.

## 3 · Mentholstein in Anwendung – `menthol-frische.mp4`
Theme-Feld: **Video + Text (Mentholstein) → Video** · Format 4:5 (oder 9:16 für Social) · Dauer 10 s

### PROMPT
```
10-second vertical 4:5 wellness product video, no text. A relaxed adult in a soft white linen shirt, seen from the side and slightly behind, face only partially visible with eyes closed. Bright Scandinavian interior, soft morning light, cool mint-green and cream color grade. The menthol stone stick exactly as in the reference image (clear crystal tip, dark green holder).

SHOT 1 (00:00-00:02) — Product Reveal
• EFFECT: Slow rack focus + shallow depth of field
• Close-up of a hand holding the menthol stone; the crystal tip catches the light
• Focus pulls from the blurred background onto the crystal tip
• Static camera

SHOT 2 (00:02-00:05) — Application
• EFFECT: Slow tracking move (left to right) + slow-motion (approximately 50% speed)
• The hand draws the crystal tip slowly along the side of the neck, below the hairline
• Camera tracks alongside the movement at the same speed
• Shoulders visibly drop and relax

SHOT 3 (00:05-00:08) — Cool Wave
• EFFECT: Subtle cool light bloom + faint mist particles + slow-motion (approximately 30% speed)
• A very soft, cool mint-tinted glow and a few fine floating mist particles spread from the touched skin outward
• The person exhales slowly; a fine strand of hair moves in the air
• This is the SIGNATURE VISUAL EFFECT — the cooling sensation made visible, kept subtle and realistic, not magical
• Transition: the glow softly fades into the final frame

SHOT 4 (00:08-00:10) — Calm End
• EFFECT: Slow dolly-out + static hold
• Camera eases back to a medium shot: the person sits relaxed by a window, menthol stone resting on the table in sharp focus in the foreground
• Movement decelerates to a still final frame
```

### MASTER EFFECTS INVENTORY
1. Rack focus / shallow depth of field — used 2x (shots 1, 4) — product clarity at start and end
2. Slow-motion — used 2x (shots 2, 3) — makes the moment feel calm and deliberate
3. Tracking move — used 1x (shot 2) — follows the application gesture
4. Cool light bloom + mist particles — used 1x (shot 3) — signature visualisation of the cooling effect
5. Slow dolly-out + static hold — used 1x (shot 4) — resolves into a clean product end frame

### EFFECTS DENSITY MAP
```
00:00-00:02 = LOW DENSITY (rack focus, shallow DOF — 2 effects in 2s)
00:02-00:05 = MEDIUM DENSITY (tracking, slow-motion — 2 effects in 3s)
00:05-00:08 = HIGH DENSITY (light bloom, mist particles, slow-motion, fade transition — 4 effects in 3s)
00:08-00:10 = LOW DENSITY (dolly-out, static hold — 2 effects in 2s)
```

### ENERGY ARC
Act 1: The product is shown clearly before it is used.
Act 2: The application gesture builds to the visible cool wave, the emotional peak.
Act 3: Tension releases into stillness, ending on the product.

## 4 · Menthol-Kristalle Makro – `kristalle.mp4`
Theme-Feld: optional (Produktseite als Medien-Video oder Social) · Format 9:16 · Dauer 6 s · nahtloser Loop

### PROMPT
```
Seamless 6-second looping macro video, vertical 9:16, no text, no people. Clear, needle-shaped menthol crystals and the crystal tip of the menthol stone from the reference image on a slowly rotating turntable. Pale mint-green seamless background, cool soft studio light with one sharp rim light, crisp macro detail, clean premium look.

SHOT 1 (00:00-00:03) — Rotation
• EFFECT: Slow 360° turntable rotation (half turn in this shot) + macro lens
• Crystals rotate slowly clockwise at a constant speed
• The rim light travels across the crystal edges, creating moving sparkles and refractions
• Static camera, very shallow depth of field

SHOT 2 (00:03-00:06) — Refraction Peak and Loop
• EFFECT: Light refraction flare + continued rotation
• As the crystals turn, light briefly refracts through the largest crystal, creating a soft mint and white prism flare
• This is the SIGNATURE VISUAL EFFECT
• Rotation completes the turn and the flare fades, so the last frame matches the first exactly for a seamless loop
```

### MASTER EFFECTS INVENTORY
1. Turntable rotation — used 2x (shots 1, 2) — constant, hypnotic motion, enables the seamless loop
2. Macro lens / shallow depth of field — used 2x (shots 1, 2) — shows crystal purity
3. Moving rim light sparkle — used 1x (shot 1) — adds life to the crystal edges
4. Refraction flare — used 1x (shot 2) — signature moment

### EFFECTS DENSITY MAP
```
00:00-00:03 = MEDIUM DENSITY (rotation, macro, rim-light sparkle — 3 effects in 3s)
00:03-00:06 = MEDIUM DENSITY (rotation, macro, refraction flare — 3 effects in 3s)
```

### ENERGY ARC
Beat 1: Hypnotic rotation draws the eye in.
Beat 2: A single flare rewards attention, then the clip folds back into its start.
